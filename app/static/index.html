    Recognition

    From triangles to a real solid

    Unfacet finds the planes, bores, fillets, chamfers and threads hiding in a mesh and rebuilds them as true CAD geometry. Change hole sizes, fix stray faces, then download a STEP for Fusion, SolidWorks, Onshape or FreeCAD.

    Works best on meshes exported from CAD: Printables, Thingiverse and MakerWorld parts. Scans and sculpts mostly come out as freeform.
